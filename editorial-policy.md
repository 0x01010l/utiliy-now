*Effective August 18, 2026*

Utiliy exists to help people solve real technical problems. This page is how the sausage is made.

## Research and testing

Tutorials are written from hardware I own or can sit in front of. Command output is from those machines unless it is labeled as an example.

Product write-ups reflect at least two weeks of use. For subscriptions, I say whether I paid or used a trial.

## Writing

I do not publish unedited machine-generated drafts. Outlines are fine. The published text, diagrams, and failure notes are edited by me.

## Updates

When a guide goes stale, I change the article and add a dated **Update log** at the bottom.

If you report an error, I check it within 7 days and either fix the post or explain why the original still applies.

## Advertising and affiliates

The site may show Google AdSense after approval. Affiliate links, if any, are marked near the first mention.

I have turned down programs that required a positive review.

## Email

Reader mail can inspire a new article, with permission. I do not publish private emails without consent.
