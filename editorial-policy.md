*Effective August 17, 2025*

Tech Pulse Daily exists to help readers solve real technical problems. This policy explains how content is created and how we handle money, ads, and corrections.

## Research and testing

Every tutorial is tested on hardware I own or have physical access to. When I reference command output, it's from my machines unless labeled as an example.

Product reviews reflect at least two weeks of personal use. For subscription services, I note whether I paid out of pocket or used a trial.

## AI and writing

I do not publish raw AI-generated drafts. Some articles begin as rough outlines in my notes app; the final text is written and edited by me, including screenshots, log snippets, and the failure sections.

## Updates and corrections

Technology changes. When a guide becomes outdated — new router UI, deprecated flag, pricing change — I update the article and add an **Update log** at the bottom with the date and what changed.

If you report an error, I verify it within 7 days and either fix the post or explain why the original text still applies.

## Advertising and affiliates

This site may display Google AdSense advertisements after approval. Some articles contain affiliate links to products I recommend. Those links are marked near the first mention.

Affiliate relationships do not determine rankings. I have declined programs that required positive coverage.

## Comments and email

Reader email may inspire new articles (with permission). I do not publish private emails without consent.
