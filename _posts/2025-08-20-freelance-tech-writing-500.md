Medium's partner program paid me **$11.40** in February 2025 for a post with 4,200 views. Same month, a boring 1,800-word router guide for a networking vendor's blog paid **$350**.

March 2025 total freelance writing: **$512**. This is how that happened — no course upsell at the end.

## Income sources that month

| Client type | Pieces | Pay |
|-------------|--------|-----|
| B2B SaaS blog (network monitoring) | 2 | $350 + $175 |
| Trade site reprint fee | 1 | $75 |
| Newsletter sponsor mention (disclosed) | 1 | $112 |
| Medium | 3 posts | $0 (literally — views below threshold) |

The SaaS pieces were **work-for-hire**. I don't own them. This site hosts **my** originals only.

## How I got the first paid gig

Not cold spam. Steps:

1. Wrote three **detailed posts here** with command output (Pi-hole, VLAN basics)
2. Linked GitHub gists with config snippets — proof I do the work
3. Commented thoughtfully on a monitoring company's engineering blog (two comments, not "hire me")
4. Their content lead emailed asking for a guest post on SNMP traps for home labbers — niche, perfect fit

Rate negotiation: they offered $250. I asked **$350** citing diagram + testing on real gear. They said yes. Always ask.

## Pitch template that worked once

Subject: `Home lab SNMP article for [Company] blog?`

> Hi [Name] —
>
> I maintain a home UniFi + Proxmox lab and wrote about Pi-hole stats last month ([link]). Your post on alert fatigue missed home-lab scale — I could cover SNMP + Grafana for under $50 hardware, 1,800 words, original screenshots.
>
> Previous work: [link] [link]
>
> — Mike

Short. Specific gap. No life story.

## What failed before March

- **Upwork lowball posts:** "$15 for 2,000 words on best VPNs" — ignored
- **Fiverr race to bottom:** listed gig, zero orders in 60 days
- **Affiliate-only blogging:** earned $8 from Amazon links on a comparison post — not worth tone compromise

## Time investment

March writing hours (~22 total):

- Research/testing: 9 hrs
- Drafting: 8 hrs
- Client revisions: 3 hrs
- Admin/invoicing: 2 hrs

Effective rate ~$23/hr — below my day job, above UserTesting for skilled work.

## Tools I use (unexciting)

- **Invoicing:** Wave (free)
- **Contracts:** client's paper; for my own sponsors, one-page Google Doc
- **Writing:** Obsidian local markdown → paste to client CMS
- **Tracking:** Spreadsheet with client, word count, rate, payment date

## Should you try this?

**Yes if:** you already produce credible technical tutorials and can hit deadlines.

**No if:** you need money this week — first pitch-to-pay took me **six weeks**.

This site is partly a **portfolio**. AdSense might add passive income later; freelance paid bills first.
