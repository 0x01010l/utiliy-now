## Key findings - **Shopify DTC leads AI visibility** in this sample — independent merchants with full Product JSON-LD and visible specs score higher than marketplace listings that gate product HTML. - **Amazon listings score well on schema** when ASIN data is extractable, but **Google SEO and keyword alignment lag** (generic page titles like “Amazon.com” reduce extractable signals). - **Best Buy, eBay, and Etsy** frequently **block or degrade automated access** — a material gap for merchants benchmarking against “leading retailer” PDPs with third-party tools. - **Keyword pillar scores are universally low** on marketplace PDPs (avg {{ agg.keywords_avg }}/100) because title/H1 alignment with body copy is weak or bot-walled. - **Top UPRS failures:** {% for rule in agg.top_uprs_rules limit:3 %}`{{ rule.id }}` ({{ rule.count }} pages){% unless forloop.last %}, {% endunless %}{% endfor %} — missing decision evidence and thin content dominate. ## Full sample table
