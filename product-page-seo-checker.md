## Why product-page SEO is different

Category pages fight for broad terms. **Product pages win long-tail, high-intent queries** — model numbers, sizes, use cases, and comparison modifiers.

This checker focuses on the on-page signals that matter for PDPs:

- Unique, descriptive title tag (not just the product name repeated everywhere)
- Meta description that matches search intent
- One clear H1 aligned with the product
- Canonical URL to prevent duplicate indexing
- Enough visible copy for buyers and parsers (not a single bullet list)
- Image alt text on primary gallery images

## Common PDP SEO mistakes we flag

1. **Title copied from the supplier** — identical to 50 other stores
2. **Missing meta description** — Google writes your snippet for you
3. **Multiple H1s** from theme widgets
4. **Thin descriptions** — specs hidden in tabs crawlers barely see
5. **No canonical** on variant URLs
