## TL;DR

| | **Surfer SEO** | **Utiliy** |
|---|----------------|------------|
| **Primary use** | Blog posts & content editor workflows | Single product detail page (PDP) |
| **Input** | Target keyword + draft text | Live product URL |
| **Output** | Content score, term suggestions, AI article drafts | Six pillar scores + copy-paste theme/CMS fixes |
| **Schema & gallery** | Not a PDP schema auditor | Product JSON-LD, images, alt text gaps |
| **AI shopping / GEO** | Indirect (content quality) | Dedicated AI visibility pillar |
| **Best for** | Editorial & SEO content teams | Ecommerce merchants & PDP launches |

Both tools can coexist — Surfer for **content marketing**, Utiliy for **product listings**.

## What Surfer SEO does well

Surfer is a strong **content optimization** platform:

- Content Editor with real-time SEO scoring  
- SERP-based keyword and structure recommendations  
- AI Writer for long-form articles and briefs  
- Workflows tuned for **blog posts and landing pages**  

If your bottleneck is “write a 2,000-word buying guide that ranks,” Surfer is in its element.

## Where Surfer is not built for PDPs

Product detail pages are not long-form articles. They are **structured commerce surfaces** with:

- Theme-controlled title and meta fields  
- Product JSON-LD and Offer markup  
- Variant URLs and canonical rules  
- Image galleries with alt text requirements  
- SKU-level facts AI shopping agents must parse  

Surfer does not crawl your live Shopify PDP and return “paste this `<meta>` tag” or “your Product schema is missing `availability`.”

## What Utiliy does instead

Utiliy is an **AI product page optimizer**:

1. Paste a public product URL  
2. Extract real data from Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, or headless JSON-LD  
3. Score **six pillars**: Google SEO, AI visibility, content, keywords, images, schema  
4. Open an **AI lab** with prioritized, copy-paste fixes  

No article outline. No word-count targets. PDP-specific evidence.

## When to use which

**Choose Surfer when:**

- You are optimizing blog content, category guides, or editorial landing pages  
- Your team lives in Google Docs / Content Editor workflows  
- Keyword density and SERP structure for **articles** is the goal  

**Choose Utiliy when:**

- You are launching or refreshing a **product listing**  
- You need schema, meta, and image fixes for a specific SKU  
- You care about **AI shopping readiness** and GEO alongside Google SEO  
- You want fixes you can paste into a theme, CMS, or marketplace backend  

**Use both when:**

- Surfer powers your **content hub**  
- Utiliy audits **hero SKUs and competitor PDPs** before ad spend
