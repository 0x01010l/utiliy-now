## Search is changing — product pages must be machine-readable

AI shopping assistants do not browse like humans. They extract **entities and facts**: name, category, price, availability, dimensions, materials, compatibility, warranty, shipping, returns.

If those facts are missing, buried in images, or inconsistent with schema, the product is harder to recommend — even if the page looks fine to a human.

## What we score

Utiliy estimates AI shopping readiness from:

- Structured Product/Offer schema
- Visible specifications and FAQs
- Brand, SKU, and variant clarity
- Shipping and returns language
- Image-supported claims (where detectable)

Example gaps we commonly find:

- Weight and dimensions missing for shipping-sensitive categories
- Compatibility lists only in PDFs or images
- Price in schema but not visible (or vice versa)
- Generic descriptions with no use-case language

## This is not "AI SEO hype"

We do not promise placement in ChatGPT or Perplexity. We measure whether **the page gives an automated system enough structured, consistent information** to describe the product accurately.
