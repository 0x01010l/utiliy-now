FAQ

Common questions

What stores does Utiliy support?

Any public product page URL. We have enhanced extractors for Shopify, headless commerce, and standard JSON-LD markup. WooCommerce and custom stores work via page markup and structured data.

How does the free audit work?

You get one complete report free — tracked by both your browser and IP address so the limit can't be bypassed by clearing cookies or switching networks alone.

What's included in Pro?

Pro is $15/month and includes 80 full audits per month, AI content analysis, Azure Vision on all product images, and priority processing. Usage is tracked in your account dashboard.

Do you store my product pages?

We fetch and analyze pages in real time to generate your report. We don't permanently archive your page content. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who operates Utiliy?

Utiliy is a brand operated by Fix Figures LLC, based in North Canton, Ohio. Contact us at contact@utiliy.com.
