## What this tool checks

Utiliy crawls a single product detail page and scores it across eight categories: technical health, on-page SEO, structured data, product information completeness, images, AI shopping readiness, content quality, and conversion clarity.

Unlike site-wide crawlers built for enterprise SEO teams, this auditor is built for **one high-intent page** — the URL that should rank and convert.

## Who it's for

- Shopify and WooCommerce merchants refreshing PDPs
- SEO freelancers auditing client product pages
- Brands benchmarking competitor listings
- Teams preparing for AI shopping and agentic commerce
