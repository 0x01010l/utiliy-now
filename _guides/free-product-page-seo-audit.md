“Free SEO audit” tools range from useless vanity scores to genuinely useful PDP diagnostics. Here is what a **free product page audit** should include — and how Utiliy’s free tier maps to it.

## What you should get for free

A credible free pass on a **single product URL** should report on:

1. **Title & meta** — length, presence, duplication risk  
2. **Headings** — H1 alignment with product name  
3. **Canonical & indexation** — obvious blockers only  
4. **Product JSON-LD** — presence, validity, Offer fields  
5. **Product facts** — price, SKU, brand, availability in HTML or schema  
6. **Images** — gallery count, missing alt text  
7. **Keyword alignment** — title vs body coverage  
8. **AI readiness** — missing attributes machines need to recommend the product  

If a tool only returns a single number with no evidence, skip it.
