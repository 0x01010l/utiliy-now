## Technical & indexation

1. Returns HTTP 200 on the canonical product URL  
2. Canonical tag points to the preferred version (watch variant URLs)  
3. Not blocked by robots.txt or noindex for Googlebot  
4. Critical product content in initial HTML (not JS-only)  
5. Page loads acceptably on mobile (Core Web Vitals)  

## On-page SEO

6. Unique title tag (~50–60 characters, product + attribute + brand)  
7. Meta description ~120–155 characters with click intent  
8. One clear H1 matching the product name  
9. Logical H2/H3 for specs, shipping, FAQs  
10. Unique body copy — no factory duplicate used across resellers  
11. Internal links from category/collection pages where relevant  

## Structured data

12. Valid Product JSON-LD  
13. Offer with price, currency, and availability  
14. Brand, SKU, GTIN, or MPN when applicable  
15. Schema price/stock matches visible page and cart  
16. No conflicting schema blocks from apps + theme  

## Product information

17. Dimensions and weight when the category expects them  
18. Materials, compatibility, or variant clarity  
19. Warranty and returns policy linked or summarized  
20. Shipping expectations stated above the fold or in specs  

## Images

21. At least two gallery images on primary SKUs  
22. Descriptive alt text on every product image  
23. Images support key claims (not decorative-only)  
24. Filenames and captions reinforce product keywords naturally  

## AI shopping & GEO (2026)

25. Product facts machines need: who it’s for, use case, constraints  
26. Structured attributes match what AI agents surface in answers  
27. No ambiguous pricing between schema, page, and checkout  
28. FAQ or specs block targeting long-tail queries you want to win

## Quick scorecard

| Pillar | Pass if… |
|--------|----------|
| Google SEO | Title, meta, H1, canonical clean |
| Schema | Valid Product + Offer |
| Keywords | Primary term in title + body |
| Images | Alt text complete |
| AI visibility | Attributes + clarity for agents |
| Content | Unique, benefit-led copy |
